<section id="travel" class="band"><div>
<div class="eyebrow">Travel</div>
<h2>Getting here, staying, exploring</h2>
<div class="chips"><a href="#get">Getting here</a><a href="#stay">Where to stay</a><a href="#dinner">Friday dinner</a><a href="#todo">Things to do</a></div>

<div class="sub" id="get"><h3>Getting here</h3>
<ul><li><b>By air:</b> fly into Lisbon (LIS). The beach is on the south side of the Tagus, so plan roughly 40 to 60 minutes by road, more at rush hour. Please check the exact time on your map app.</li>
<li><b>Rental car:</b> pick up at the airport and book early, June is busy. Portuguese motorways use electronic tolls, so ask the rental company for a toll device.</li>
<li><b>From Córdoba (and southern Spain):</b> about 6 hours by road. Option 1: via Badajoz and Évora, then towards Lisbon. Option 2: via Seville and Huelva, then the A22 along the Algarve and north. Check both on a map app before you leave.</li>
<li><b>Between places:</b> a car or taxi is easiest. We are looking at a shuttle between hotels and the wedding venue, details to follow.</li></ul></div>

<div class="sub" id="stay"><h3>Where to stay</h3>
<p>We suggest staying in Azeitão, around Aroeira, or somewhere in between. Setúbal and Sesimbra are too far. Please book early.</p>
<div class="cols">
<div class="card"><small>Azeitão</small><p>Close to the wedding venue. Look at the hotel Turim Club d'Azeitão, or guesthouses and quintas in the village.</p></div>
<div class="card"><small>In between</small><p>The Pousada Castelo de Palmela, or apartments and villas in the surrounding area.</p></div>
<div class="card"><small>Aroeira / Fonte da Telha</small><p>Close to Friday's drinks and the beach, with villas and apartments to rent.</p></div></div></div>

<div class="sub" id="dinner"><h3>Friday dinner (on your own)</h3>
<p>Friday is drinks and bites only, so here are ideas for dinner nearby, from relaxed to special. Book ahead.</p>
<ul><li><b>Relaxed and budget-friendly:</b> OhhhTC, an honest neighbourhood place in Costa da Caparica.</li>
<li><b>Mid-range, on the beach:</b> Piscina in Fonte da Telha (dishes to share), or Sotavento for seafood.</li>
<li><b>A bit more special:</b> a beach lounge such as Casablanca Beach Lounge, or a table in Lisbon.</li></ul></div>

<div class="sub" id="todo"><h3>Things to do if you stay longer</h3>
<ul><li><b>Lisbon:</b> Alfama, Belém, the viewpoints, LX Factory and the Time Out Market. A ferry from Cacilhas is a nice way in.</li>
<li><b>Aroeira and Costa da Caparica:</b> the beaches, the seafront boardwalk, surf lessons.</li>
<li><b>Azeitão and Arrábida:</b> the nature park and its beaches, Azeitão cheese and tarts, wine cellars, Palmela castle.</li></ul></div>
</div></section>
